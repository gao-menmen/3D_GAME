# Third-Party Asset Policy

- The repository must remain private whenever it contains source assets from Epic, Fab, Marketplace, or paid third-party providers.
- Every third-party asset must be recorded in `ThirdParty/AssetRegistry.csv` before it is imported.
- Assets may be released only as cooked product output and only when the applicable license permits that distribution.
- Never publish third-party source assets, credentials, receipts, account identifiers, or local absolute paths.
- Lyra Starter Game 5.8 must be obtained through Epic Games Launcher or Fab. Its content may be distributed externally only as cooked output permitted by the Epic Content License.
